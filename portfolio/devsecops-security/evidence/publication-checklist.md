# Evidence Publication Checklist

This directory is part of a student's job-search portfolio, **not** a full release of university/team security assessment records.

Before making any additional material public:

- [ ] Confirm permission to share each team document, screenshot or diagram.
- [ ] Remove student IDs, staff contacts, customer data, internal addresses, tokens and credentials.
- [ ] Check screenshots for hidden secret values, scan output and embedded URLs.
- [ ] Keep evidence dates explicit and distinguish historical from current scan results.
- [ ] Verify claims against commits, runs or retained screenshots.
- [ ] Describe contributions honestly (implemented, assisted, reviewed, verified).
- [ ] Avoid uploading full internal reports without express permission.
- [ ] Review the October 12 Gitleaks alert securely; rotate/revoke any confirmed real credentials.
- [ ] Do not upload binary security reports or SARIF until reviewed for sensitive content.
- [ ] Do not disclose unresolved exploit details beyond what is already appropriately public.
